Love

Aapse mulaqat hui,
To aisa laga jaise barsaat hui,
Us barsaat ki boondon mein hum aise bheege,
Ab dhoop bhi keh rahi hai,kya baat hui?

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Love

khanjar toh yun hi badnaam hai,jurm toh in aankhon ka hai,
Qaidi hain ye zulfein teri,naam badnaam salakhon ka hai,
Qatl kar de ada-e-husn tera
Aur ilzaam in be-kasoor haathon ka hai

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Sad

Intezaar inteqaal ho gaya dekhte-dekhte,
Saara qissa fizool-e-haal ho gaya dekhte-dekhte,
Woh jo kal dariya mein dooba tha, aaj paani tak naseeb nahi,
Samandar ko gumaan ho gaya dekhte-dekhte

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Sad

Dhalta raha saanche mein, saanchaa hi toot gaya,
Badalta raha khud ko, khud se hi rooth gaya,
Bahut tavajjo se sunna chahta hoon
Tumhara shubha(shaq) karna, hamara saara sunna chhoot gaya

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Life

Tamanna hai ki yun hi chalta rahun,
Sabhi ki aankhon mein yun hi khalta rahun
Talluqaat zaroori pad jaate hain, warna
Dil kehta hai ki yun hi marta rahun

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Life

Fazeehat ki baatein shehzade nahi karte,
Gulzaar se gul-chini ke iraade nahi karte
Yun to zamaana hai fareb ka,
Par jahan bharosa hota hai, wahan vaade nahi karte

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Life

Waqt guzar raha hai, par guzar nahi raha,
Shaam dhal rahi hai, din ubhar raha hai, par ubhar nahi raha,
Dil-e-tamanna mein uljha hua hai,
Haalat-e-daur sudhar raha hai, par sudhar nahi raha

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Life

Intezaar kar raha hoon, karne do, waqt zaya kar chuke,
Tasavvur se dekh raha hoon, dekhne do, aankhein haya kar chuke,
Saadgi, shaukat, shiddat—sab farebi hain
Khud mein doob raha hoon, doobne do, khud se saaya kar chuke

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Attitude

Meri khamoshiyon se khamosh na samajhna
Tufan lane se pahle patte bhi saham jate hai

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
Attitude

Girte-girte sambhala hoon, ab giroonga nahi,
Raaste wahi hain, par un par chalunga nahi,
Ab chahe udkar jaana ho ya tair kar
Lekin un raaton par mudunga nahi

~Yuvraj Ki Lekhini
