Yuvraj Singh — Jeevani

Yuvraj Singh Uttar Pradesh ke Shahjahanpur jile ke Powayan ke rehne wale ek yuva aur ubharte hue rachnakar hain. Unka janm 29 August 2009 ko hua. Unhe shabdon ke saath samay bitana, apne vicharon ko likhna aur apni bhavnaon ko alfaazon ka roop dena pasand hai. Yuvraj ka lekhan kisi ek nirdharit sahityik pehchan tak simit nahi hai. Woh mukhyatah shayari aur kavitaen likhte hain aur apni rachnaon ke madhyam se un bhavnaon ko vyakt karne ki koshish karte hain jo aksar seedhe shabdon mein keh pana mushkil hota hai.

Lekhan ki shuruaat

Yuvraj ko likhne ka shauk lagbhag 15 saal ki umr mein aur adhik gehra hua. Shuruaat mein likhna unke liye ek shauk tha, lekin dheere-dheere yah unke liye apne vicharon aur bhavnaon ko samajhne aur vyakt karne ka ek madhyam ban gaya. Unki writing kisi ek kalpanik duniya tak simit nahi hai. Yuvraj ke anusar, unhone ab tak jo bhi likha hai, usmein unki vaastavik feelings ka anubhav juda hua hai. Isi wajah se unki rachnaon mein vyaktigat bhavnaon, khwahishon, rishton, zindagi aur man ki baaton ko ek swabhavik roop mein dekha ja sakta hai. Unke liye likhna keval sundar shabd likhne ka naam nahi hai. Woh apni bhavnaon ko samajhne, unhe shabdon mein dhalne aur phir un shabdon ko doosron tak pahunchane ki koshish karte hain.

Yuvraj ka lekhan ke prati nazariya

Yuvraj apne aap ko abhi kisi badi sahityik upadhi se nahi jodte. Woh khud ko na to lekhak hone ka daava karte hain, na kavi aur na hi shayar hone ka. Unke liye sabse sahi parichay shayad yahi hai ki— "Na main lekhak hoon, na kavi hoon, na shayar hoon; bas likhne ki koshish kar raha hoon." Yahi soch unke lekhan ko ek alag swabhav deti hai. Woh kisi upadhi ke peeche nahi, balki apne alfaazon ko behtar banane ke peeche chalna chahte hain. Unka maanna hai ki likhne wala vyakti har rachna ke saath kuch naya seekhta hai. Isi liye Yuvraj apni writing ko ek poori ho chuki manzil nahi, balki lagataar chalne wali yatra ke roop mein dekhte hain.

Dil-e-Khwahish

Yuvraj ki rachnatmak soch ko "Dil-e-Khwahish" shabd ke madhyam se samjha ja sakta hai. Is naam mein unke liye dil ki woh khwahishein, bhavnaen aur baatein shamil hain jinhe woh apne alfaazon ke zariye vyakt karna chahte hain. Unki rachnaon mein kabhi khushi, kabhi udaasi, kabhi mohabbat, kabhi dooriyan aur kabhi zindagi ke saamanya ehsaas apna roop lete hain. Lekin in sabke beech ek baat saamanya hai — bhavnaon ko sachchai ke saath shabdon mein rakhne ki koshish.

Yuvraj Ki Lekhini

Apni writing ko ek vyavasthit aur adhik professional roop mein logon tak pahunchane ke uddeshya se Yuvraj ne "Yuvraj Ki Lekhini" naam se apna personal writing platform shuru kiya. "Yuvraj Ki Lekhini" keval unki shayari aur kavitaon ko rakhne ka ek madhyam nahi hai, balki yah unke liye apni rachnatmak pehchan banane ki shuruaat bhi hai. "Lekhini" shabd ka sambandh kalam se hai, aur Yuvraj ke liye kalam un bhavnaon ko vyakt karne ka madhyam hai jinhe shayad saamne se kehna hamesha sambhav nahi hota. Is platform ke madhyam se Yuvraj apni rachnaon ko adhik logon tak pahunchana, naye logon se judna aur apni writing ke madhyam se ek alag pehchan banana chahte hain.

Rachnaon ki prastuti

Yuvraj apni rachnaon ko school ke karyakramon mein bhi prastut kar chuke hain. Ye anubhav unke liye apne likhe hue alfaazon ko keval kagaz ya screen tak simit na rakhkar logon ke saamne prastut karne ka ek avsar raha hai. Abhi tak unhe koi aupcharik sahityik puraskar ya bada samman prapt nahi hua hai. Phir bhi Yuvraj apni writing ko lekar aage badhne aur apne lekhan ko samay ke saath aur behtar banane ki ichha rakhte hain.

Bhavishya ki soch

Yuvraj writing ko apne jeevan ka ek aisa hissa banana chahte hain jise woh samay ke saath aur gehra kar saken. Unka uddeshya keval kuch rachnaen likh dena nahi, balki dheere-dheere apni ek alag writing style aur pehchan banana hai. Bhavishya mein woh apni rachnaon ka ek sangrah ya pustak prakashit karne ki ichha rakhte hain. Saath hi, woh apni writing ke madhyam se aise logon tak pahunchna chahte hain jo unke alfaazon mein apni feelings ka koi hissa mehsoos kar saken. Yuvraj ke liye safalta ka arth keval kisi samman ya upadhi ko prapt karna nahi hai. Unke liye safalta tab hogi jab unke likhe hue kuch alfaaz kisi doosre vyakti ke dil ko chhoo saken, kisi ankahe ehsaas ko awaaz de saken ya kisi vyakti ko yah mehsoos kara saken ki jo woh mehsoos kar raha hai, use kisi aur ne bhi kabhi mehsoos kiya hai.

Ek rachnatmak yatra

Yuvraj Singh ki kahani abhi kisi poori hui sahityik yatra ki kahani nahi hai. Yah ek aise yuva ki shuruaat hai jise likhne ka shauk hai aur jo apni bhavnaon ko shabdon mein dhalne ki lagataar koshish kar raha hai. Woh apne liye bade-bade daave nahi karte. Unki pehchan filhaal unki koshish, unke alfaaz aur unki rachnaon se ban rahi hai. Unke liye "Yuvraj Ki Lekhini" sirf ek naam nahi, balki us safar ka ek hissa hai jahan ek shauk dheere-dheere ek pehchan ka roop le raha hai. Aur shayad Yuvraj ki sabse sahi pehchan bhi yahi hai— "Na main lekhak hoon, na kavi hoon, na shayar hoon; bas apne dil ki baaton ko alfaaz dene ki koshish kar raha hoon." Yahi koshish unki writing ki shuruaat hai, aur yahi unki "Yuvraj Ki Lekhini" ki kahani bhi.
