About Yuvraj

Yuvraj is the creator of Yuvraj Ki Lekhini, a personal writing platform dedicated to original shayari, poems and heartfelt words.

Through writing, Yuvraj expresses emotions, thoughts, experiences and different shades of life in Hindi and Hinglish.

"Alfaaz jo dil se nikle, wahi Yuvraj Ki Lekhini ban gaye."

Yuvraj Ki Lekhini

Yuvraj Ki Lekhini is a personal collection of shayari and poems written by Yuvraj. The platform brings together words about love, emotions, life, memories and feelings.
